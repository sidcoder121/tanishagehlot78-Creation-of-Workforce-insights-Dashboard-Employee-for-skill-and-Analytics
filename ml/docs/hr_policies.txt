=========================================
TALENT INTELLIGENCE CORP - HR POLICIES & HANDBOOK
=========================================

SECTION 1: PROFESSIONAL TRAINING AND SKILL DEVELOPMENT POLICY
Talent Intelligence Corp is committed to employee learning and development. Every full-time employee is eligible for professional training.
- Funding Limit: The company provides up to $5,000 annually per employee for approved external certifications, coursework, or conferences.
- Required Training Hours: All employees are expected to complete a minimum of 16 training hours per calendar year.
- Training Times limit: Employees may attend up to 6 structured training events or courses per year during standard working hours.
- Skill Gaps: Employees flagged with a Skill Gap (low performance rating and low training hours) will be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) and assigned a dedicated mentor. They are mandated to complete 3 specific skill-building training courses in the subsequent quarter.

SECTION 2: WORK-LIFE BALANCE AND OVERTIME GUIDELINES
We believe that sustainable productivity requires a healthy balance between personal life and professional duties.
- Core Working Hours: Our standard core working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
- Overtime Policy: Overtime must be pre-approved by the department manager. Standard hours are 80 hours per bi-weekly pay period. Any overtime hours exceeding 10 hours a week will trigger an automatic work-life balance alert to HR.
- Remote Work: Full-time employees are eligible for hybrid work, allowing up to 2 days of remote work per week, subject to team performance and manager approval.
- Rest and Recreation: Employees are encouraged to take at least 15 consecutive days of annual leave to recharge.

SECTION 3: PROMOTION AND CAREER ADVANCEMENT
Career advancement at Talent Intelligence Corp is based on performance, tenure, and leadership potential.
- Promotion Readiness Check: To be eligible for a promotion review, an employee must satisfy:
  1. Have at least 3 years since their last promotion.
  2. Have a Performance Rating of 3 (Excellent) or 4 (Outstanding) in their most recent performance review.
  3. Be currently below Job Level 5 (Executive level).
- Review Cycle: Promotion reviews are conducted bi-annually in June and December.
- Job Levels: Job levels range from Level 1 (Entry-level Associate) to Level 5 (Director/VP).

SECTION 4: RETENTION AND WORKFORCE WELLNESS PROGRAM
We offer several retention incentives to keep our workforce healthy, satisfied, and highly motivated.
- Stock Options: Equity grants (Stock Option Level 1 to 3) are awarded to key contributors based on annual performance and job level.
- Wellness Allowance: A wellness stipend of $150 per month is provided to cover gym memberships, mental health apps, or sports equipment.
- Sabbatical Leave: Employees with over 7 years of tenure (Long-Term Employees) are eligible for a 4-week fully paid sabbatical.
- Retention Bonus: In high-risk departments (e.g. Sales, or specific technical research units), HR may offer a retention bonus of up to 15% of annual base salary in exchange for a 1-year service commitment.
